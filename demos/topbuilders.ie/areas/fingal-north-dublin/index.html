Local context

Building in Fingal & North Dublin.

The area spans mature north-city neighbourhoods, coastal towns, established estates and lower-density sites. That means project types range from terraced renovations to larger additions and one-off homes.

Fingal County Council requirements, site context, drainage, access and any previous planning permissions should be reviewed for the specific property.
