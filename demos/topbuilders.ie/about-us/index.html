About Top Builders

Construction decisions, made visible.

Top Builders is the residential construction identity of Vascons Property Solutions Limited, based in Sandyford and serving Dublin, Wicklow, Kildare and surrounding areas.

Start a project brief
The position

The homeowner should understand what happens next.

Residential construction is a significant commitment. Our process starts with the practical questions: whether the project fits, what information is available, which site conditions shape the scope, and what decisions need to happen next.

Top Builders operates through Vascons Property Solutions Limited, company number 763135, from 6 Fern Road, Sandyford Business Park, Dublin, D18 FP98.

Working principles

Specific enough to guide the build.

  • Understand the property before fixing the construction route
  • Coordinate new work with the existing home
  • Keep scope, responsibilities and next decisions clear
  • Bring structure, services and finishes into one programme
  • Communicate progress in practical, homeowner-friendly language
Project route

A straightforward structure for complex work.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Tell us what needs to change and what the finished home should make possible.

  2. 02

    Site visit

    We look at access, structure, constraints and the existing fabric before shaping the scope.

  3. 03

    Scope

    The work, assumptions and next decisions are set out clearly before construction begins.

  4. 04

    Build

    A coordinated team delivers the agreed work with practical communication throughout.

  5. 05

    Handover

    We walk the finished work with you and close out the agreed project details.
