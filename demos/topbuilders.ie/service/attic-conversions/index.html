Common questions

Before planning attic conversions.

01Is every attic suitable for conversion?

No. Roof geometry, structure, headroom, stair position and compliance requirements can make some options impractical or require a more substantial roof intervention.

02Does a dormer need planning permission?

The answer depends on the property, location and proposal. Confirm the planning position with the relevant professional and local authority guidance.

03Can an ensuite be included?

Potentially. Drainage routes, water pressure, ventilation, headroom and the room below all need to work together.
