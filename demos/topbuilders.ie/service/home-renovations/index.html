Common questions

Before planning major renovations.

01Can you renovate one floor while we live elsewhere in the house?

Sometimes, but dust, noise, service shutdowns and safe separation must be assessed honestly. Large structural or services projects are usually more efficient in an empty work area.

02How are hidden conditions handled?

Known risks should be identified during surveys and opening-up where appropriate. If concealed conditions emerge, their effect on scope and sequence should be documented before related work proceeds.

03Can energy upgrades be included?

Yes. Fabric and service improvements often make sense during major renovation, provided they are designed as a coordinated performance strategy.
