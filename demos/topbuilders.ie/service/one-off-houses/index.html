Common questions

Before planning one-off houses.

01Do you provide architectural design?

The construction company works with the client’s appropriately appointed architect, engineer and other consultants. Roles should be clear before pricing and mobilisation.

02When should a builder join the project?

Early contractor input can help with buildability and scope, but meaningful pricing requires coordinated information. The right timing depends on the procurement route.

03Can finishes be selected during the build?

Some can, but late decisions create programme and cost risk. A decision schedule should identify long-lead and construction-critical selections.
