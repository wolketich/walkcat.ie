A brighter ground floor, carefully connected.

Area
Greater Dublin
Type
Rear extension & internal reconfiguration
The brief

Start with the change the home needs to make.

A ground-floor extension works hardest when the new space improves the rooms already there. The approach connects kitchen, dining and garden while protecting useful storage and circulation.

01 / Challenge

Deep plans can leave the centre of the home dark and turn circulation into wasted floor area. Structure, drainage, access and the everyday family route all need to be solved together.

02 / Response

A coordinated opening strategy, carefully placed roof glazing and a clear material transition can bring light deeper into the plan and make the extension feel like part of the original home.
