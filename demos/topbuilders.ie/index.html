Core disciplines

Improve the whole home—not just the new square metres.

Top Builders focuses on substantial residential work where new structure, existing rooms, services and finishes have to come together.

01House Extensions

Considered house extensions across Greater Dublin, planned around your home, access, budget priorities and day-to-day family life.

02Kitchen Extensions

Kitchen and rear extensions designed around daylight, circulation, storage, structure and the way your household uses the ground floor.

03Terraced Extensions

Extension and reconfiguration work for Dublin terraces, with close attention to access, neighbouring properties, daylight and the original house.

04Two-Storey Extensions

Coordinated two-storey extensions for Greater Dublin homes that need meaningful space across both levels.

05Glass Extensions

Light-filled extensions where glazing, structure, comfort and the existing building are designed to work together.

06Major Renovations

Construction delivery for carefully designed one-off homes, coordinated with the client’s appointed architect, engineer and professional team.

First questions

Start with what you know. We can identify what is missing.

01What kind of projects are the best fit for Top Builders?

The primary focus is house extensions and substantial residential renovation across Dublin and the surrounding counties, including work where structure, services and finishes need to be coordinated as one build.

02Can I get in touch before my drawings are complete?

Yes. Use the first conversation to explain the property, what needs to change and where you are in the process. Meaningful fixed pricing still depends on sufficiently coordinated information.

03Which areas do you cover?

The primary service area covers Dublin and North Wicklow. Top Builders also considers projects across Kildare, wider Wicklow and North County Dublin.

04Can I send plans and photos with the enquiry?

Yes. The guided project form accepts up to three PDF or image files, and you can continue the conversation on WhatsApp if you have more material.
